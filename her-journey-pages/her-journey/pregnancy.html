Your Pregnancy Journey

Information and support through every stage of pregnancy.

🥗

Pregnancy Nutrition

Good nutrition during pregnancy supports the health of the mother and the developing baby. A balanced diet should provide essential nutrients, vitamins and minerals throughout pregnancy.

  • 🥬 Fruits and vegetables: Include a variety of colorful fruits and vegetables.
  • 🌾 Whole grains: Choose foods such as oats, brown rice and whole-grain bread.
  • 🥚 Protein: Include eggs, beans, lentils, nuts and well-cooked lean meats.
  • 🥛 Calcium: Milk, yogurt and other calcium-rich foods can support healthy bones and teeth.
  • 💧 Stay hydrated: Drink enough water throughout the day.
🤰

Pregnancy Changes

Pregnancy brings many physical and emotional changes. These changes can vary from person to person and may change as pregnancy progresses.

🌸 First Trimester

Fatigue, nausea, breast tenderness and increased urination are common changes.

🌷 Second Trimester

Energy may improve, the abdomen grows and the baby begins to move.

🌺 Third Trimester

Increased body size, back discomfort, tiredness and preparation for birth may occur.

💗 Emotional Changes

Mood changes and different emotions can occur because of hormonal and lifestyle changes.

🩺

Prenatal Care

Regular prenatal care helps monitor the health of both the mother and baby. Healthcare professionals can provide appropriate guidance throughout pregnancy.

  • Regular checkups: Attend appointments recommended by your healthcare provider.
  • Take prescribed supplements: Take prenatal vitamins or supplements only as recommended by your healthcare professional.
  • Monitor your health: Keep track of important changes and discuss concerns during appointments.
  • Ask questions: Talk openly with your healthcare provider about symptoms, nutrition, exercise and medicines.
❤️

Care During Pregnancy

Taking care of your physical and emotional well-being is important during pregnancy.

  • 🥗 Eat balanced meals: Choose a variety of nutritious foods.
  • 🚶 Stay active: Follow your healthcare provider's advice about safe physical activity.
  • 😴 Get enough rest: Give your body time to rest and recover.
  • 🧘 Manage stress: Relaxation, gentle activities and emotional support can help you feel better.
  • 💧 Drink water: Keep yourself hydrated throughout the day.
⚠️

Things to Avoid

Some substances and activities can be harmful during pregnancy. Always ask your healthcare provider if you are unsure about something.

  • Avoid alcohol and smoking.
  • Do not use medicines or supplements without professional advice.
  • Avoid foods that your healthcare provider has advised you to avoid.
  • Follow safety recommendations for physical activities.

💗 Remember

Every pregnancy is different. The information on this page is for general awareness and should not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional. If you have concerns about your pregnancy or symptoms that worry you, contact your healthcare provider.
