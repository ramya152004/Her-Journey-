PCOS Awareness

Understand your hormones and your health.

🌸 PCOS

Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) is a hormonal condition that can affect menstrual cycles, ovulation and hormone levels. Symptoms can vary from person to person.

🌷 PCOD

PCOD is a term commonly used for hormonal and reproductive changes involving irregular ovulation. Understanding symptoms and discussing concerns with a healthcare professional can be helpful.

🦋 Thyroid

Thyroid hormones play an important role in the body. Changes in thyroid function can sometimes affect menstrual cycles and overall wellbeing. Appropriate testing and medical advice can help identify thyroid problems.
